# 🌆 CityPulse

### *Report urban problems. Help build a better city.*

CityPulse is a web-based platform that makes it simple for people to report everyday urban problems and bring attention to issues that affect their community.

From **garbage and waterlogging to damaged roads and street-light problems**, CityPulse aims to turn small reports into meaningful change.

---

## ✨ What is CityPulse?

CityPulse provides a simple interface where users can:

📍 **Report an urban problem**
📝 **Describe the issue**
🏙️ **Specify its location**
📢 **Help bring attention to problems in their area**
